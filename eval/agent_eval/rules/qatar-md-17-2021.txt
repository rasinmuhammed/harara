Ministerial Decision No. 17 of 2021 on precautions to protect workers from heat stress (representative English excerpt; verify against the official gazette before operational use).

Article 2. During the period from 1 June to 15 September each year, work in open workplaces and workplaces exposed to direct sunlight is prohibited from 10:00 to 15:30.

Article 3. Irrespective of the period and hours stated in Article 2, work must stop whenever the wet-bulb globe temperature (WBGT) index at the workplace reaches 32.1 degrees Celsius, and may resume only when the index falls below that value.

Article 4. Employers shall provide shaded rest areas and cool drinking water, and shall organise a work-rest schedule appropriate to the measured heat load.
