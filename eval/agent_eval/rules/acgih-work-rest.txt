ACGIH threshold limit values for heat stress, screening criteria (paraphrased).

The screening criteria give the WBGT above which a work-rest cycle must be applied, by metabolic demand category. For an acclimatised worker: light work may continue at 100 percent up to 29.5 C; moderate work at 100 percent up to 27.5 C; heavy work at 100 percent up to 26.0 C. Above those values the allowable work fraction falls to 75, 50 and 25 percent of each hour as WBGT rises.

For heavy work, a 50 percent work fraction (30 minutes work per hour) applies once WBGT exceeds 26.0 C. For heavy work, a 25 percent work fraction applies once WBGT exceeds 28.5 C.

These are screening values for a healthy, adequately hydrated population and are not a substitute for physiological monitoring.
