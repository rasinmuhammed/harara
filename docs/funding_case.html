Seeing the heat casualty before it happens

The problem

One calendar rule against a physiological hazard

Migrant construction and delivery workers in Qatar face a humid-heat hazard linked to excess cardiovascular mortality (Pradhan et al., Cardiology 2019) and flagged specifically for gig workers by Human Rights Watch and a 2024 ILO convention. Qatar's Ministerial Decision 17/2021 answers it with a fixed midday ban — roughly 10:00–15:30, June to mid-September — plus a stop-work clause at WBGT 32.1 °C.

In the regulated summer window that threshold is exceeded on 58–61% of hours (16-year climatology, Liljegren WBGT). The hazard is frequent and rising. A single clock rule cannot see who is working how hard, or whether they arrived last week.

What we tested

Four ways the thesis could have been novel — each tested to destruction

Before claiming a technical moat, we tried to break the obvious ones. The record is kept in full (results_ledger.md); the load-bearing entries:

Contribution 2

The anticipatory individual heat-strain twin

A physics particle filter fuses heart rate, accelerometry and a skin-temperature patch through a two-node thermoregulation model, forced by the measured environment. It self-calibrates to the person (a latent metabolic scale), then propagates the particle cloud forward under the WBGT forecast to give P(core temperature crosses 38.5 °C within the hour).

Synthetic — not yet evidence

All physiology here is generated by a two-node rational model, not measured. The numbers are indicative. The pilot — ingestible-capsule ground truth, mixed-effects Bland–Altman primary endpoint, per-subject leave-one-out — is what converts this from method to result. Protocol: digital_twin_protocol.md.

Why this is genuinely unclaimed

USARIEM's ECTemp estimates core temperature from heart rate alone with a random-walk model and no forecast — validated on soldiers. ISO 7933 / Fiala / JOS-3 are open-loop forward simulators. No published system assimilates a physics model to a worker's live sensors and propagates it under the WBGT forecast, validated against capsule temperature, in a migrant-labour cohort.

The system

The closed loop is the product

Sense low-cost wearables — heart rate, accelerometry, skin-temperature patch
↓
Estimate physics particle filter — self-calibrating individual core temperature
↓
Anticipate forward propagation under the WBGT forecast — P(core > 38.5 °C within the hour)
↓
Schedule CVaR work/rest optimiser — per-crew chance constraint replaces the population threshold
↓
Observe & feed back realised conditions and outcomes re-tune the filter and the forecast ensemble

Contribution 1 demonstrates the scheduling arrow on real data. Contribution 2 demonstrates the estimation arrow on synthetic physiology. The pilot validates the estimation arrow and closes the loop.

The ask

What the funding buys

  • The wearable pilot. n = 30 workers × ≥ 4 sessions across the acclimatisation window, ingestible-capsule ground truth, IRB-grade ethics (individual data firewalled from the employer, non-punitive-use contract, real-time stop criteria). Converts Contribution 2 into peer-reviewable evidence.
  • GEFS Reforecast v12 — backfill and calibration. The Doha extraction pipeline and the lagged-ensemble model are built and tested; validation slices show ensemble spread that grows correctly with lead. The funded work is the 2000–2019 warm-season backfill and the per-lead bias/spread calibration (EMOS) that makes the scenarios honest — the homogeneous archive a real forecast-reliability model needs. (The public operational archive carries only temperature before 2024.)
  • Instrumented sites. The block-scale microclimate data — trench, rooftop, sun-baked steel — that public models provably cannot resolve.

Method footing

Built on established standards, tested against measurements

WBGT via the physically-based Liljegren energy balance (ECMWF thermofeel), validated globally by Kong & Huber 2022 — and against a naive formula the project scaffold used and this work discarded. Physiological limits from the ACGIH TLV / Action-Limit screening criteria (ISO 7243). Thermoregulation from the two-node Gagge–Stolwijk–Nishi model. Scheduling from Rockafellar–Uryasev CVaR optimisation. Estimation from sequential Monte Carlo (bootstrap particle filter with kernel roughening). Forecast uncertainty from analog residual resampling today, and a NOAA GEFS v12 reforecast lagged ensemble (point-extracted from S3 by GRIB byte-range) as the calibrated substrate. Ground truth from OTHH station observations — which caught a real defect in the reanalysis wind (a −1.5 to −2.5 m/s bias from November 2024, since patched). Every figure reproduces from ./run_all.sh; the physics and the leakage-safe evaluation have unit tests.

Limitations carried in full in technical_report.md §11: single station and metro; reanalysis (not ERA5) as the gridded product under test; all Contribution 2 physiology synthetic from a model that shares a family with its own filter; conservative population thresholds, not individual medical limits. Nothing here has left the laptop as a published claim.
